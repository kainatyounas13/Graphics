About Me

Hi! I'm Kainat Younas, a Professional Graphic Designer and Digital Creator with 3+ years of experience in creating creative and eye-catching designs.

I specialize in Logo Design, Business Card Design, Social Media Posts, Flyer & Poster Design, YouTube Thumbnail Design, Video Editing, and 3D Editing. My portfolio also includes selected T-Shirt design projects.

With creativity, attention to detail, and a passion for design, I turn ideas into meaningful visuals that help brands stand out. I work with tools like Adobe Photoshop, CapCut, Adobe Illustrator, and Canva to bring creative concepts to life.
